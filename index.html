01 / Project overview

Turn disconnected records into an investigation trail.

Individual transactions can look ordinary in isolation. Looking across financial, behavioural, temporal, and relationship-based signals helps surface records that deserve a closer look.

The challenge

Transaction-level checks can miss patterns across employees, suppliers, purchase orders, invoices, and payments. Analysts also need to understand why a record is flagged—not just receive a score.

◎

The approach

Combine explainable screening rules with a directed knowledge graph, indicator convergence, and a weighted investigation-priority score that helps organise manual review.

02 / Workflow

From source data to explainable cases.

A reproducible pipeline that keeps synthetic test data distinct from the original procurement backbone.

01 · PrepareProfile records, process dates and numeric fields, validate identifiers.
02 · ConnectBuild consistent PO-level entities and clearly labelled synthetic links.
03 · ScreenRun eight forensic rules and combine applicable indicators.
04 · InvestigateTrace relationships, explain score drivers, and review controlled tests.
03 / Analytical methods

Eight signals. One explainable review process.

Each method identifies a pattern that may warrant follow-up. No individual signal proves fraud.

METHOD 01

Benford’s Law

Checks population-level deviations in leading-digit distributions.

METHOD 02

Duplicate invoices

Finds repeated supplier, invoice-date, and amount combinations.

METHOD 03

IQR outliers

Surfaces unusually high purchase-order values.

METHOD 04

Employee–vendor concentration

Flags disproportionate interaction patterns for contextual review.

METHOD 05

Temporal anomalies

Looks for unusual concentrations in daily payment activity.

METHOD 06

PO–invoice variance

Compares invoice amounts against associated purchase-order values.

METHOD 07

Split purchases

Checks related sub-threshold purchases within a 30-day window.

METHOD 08

Round-number analysis

Uses exact round amounts as a supporting screening signal.

05 / Risk model

Transparent weights, visible reasons.
